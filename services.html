COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Inquiries

Clear answers about working models, timelines, and technical deliverables.

What is your typical project delivery timeline?
Standard marketing websites and brand platforms typically ship within 2 to 4 weeks. Custom fullstack portals and multi-tenant SaaS products take 6 to 10 weeks depending on complexity. We structure every engagement into transparent weekly sprint milestones.
Do I own the full intellectual property and code?
Yes, 100%. Upon final delivery, all intellectual property, design assets, Figma files, and Git repositories are transferred directly to your organization. Zero proprietary lock-in.
Do you offer post-launch support and ongoing maintenance?
Every project includes a complimentary 30-day post-launch warranty covering any bugs or minor adjustments. Afterward, we offer flexible SLA retainers covering 24/7 uptime monitoring, security patching, and feature expansions.
How do we get started?
Simply fill out our brief contact form or send a WhatsApp message. We review inquiries within 24 hours and schedule a 30-minute discovery call to discuss scope, timeline, and preliminary architecture.
